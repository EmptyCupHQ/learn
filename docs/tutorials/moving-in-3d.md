# Moving objects in 3D

To move objects in 3D:

1. Press and hold the X or Y or Z key on your keyboard.
2. Hover your mouse on the object that you want to move.
3. Depending on the key you press, the corresponding (X or Y or Z) axis is shown in 3D and the mouse pointer changes its shape.
4. Click to pick up the target object to move along the highlighted axis.
5. Move your mouse along the axis.
6. Click again to drop the object at the desired position.

!!! tip Into the screen

    Sometimes moving an object into the screen may be hard. The object may go out of control.
    Move your position slightly so that you can see the selected axis fully for easy movement.


!!! warning

    Sometimes the object may disappear when moving in 3D. This happens when the object gets moved
    out of the house. You can use **Ctrl + Z** to undo the change. You can also go into the top view
    to see where the object was moved.
